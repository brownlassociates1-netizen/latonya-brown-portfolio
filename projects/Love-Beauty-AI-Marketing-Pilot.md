# Love & Beauty by Dajahnay — AI Marketing Pilot

## AI-Powered Marketing and Client-Growth System

The Love & Beauty by Dajahnay AI Marketing Pilot demonstrates how a service-based beauty business can use artificial intelligence, automation, and digital marketing tools to attract new clients, promote services, and maintain stronger customer relationships.

**Video demonstration:** [Watch the AI Marketing Pilot](https://youtu.be/pHB9LnhGz8o)

## The Business Problem

Independent beauty professionals often manage appointments, customer communication, social media, promotions, and follow-up while also providing services to clients. This makes consistent marketing difficult and can cause potential clients to be missed.

The pilot was designed to create a more organized client-growth system that reduces manual marketing work and helps the business reach more potential customers.

## My Role

I developed the AI marketing concept, business requirements, customer journey, automation strategy, feature plan, marketing workflow, and video demonstration.

I designed the solution around the actual needs of a beauty business, with a focus on generating leads, increasing appointments, improving customer engagement, and encouraging repeat business.

## Proposed Customer Journey

1. A potential client discovers the business through digital marketing or social media.
2. The system directs the client to service and appointment information.
3. The client’s interest is captured for appropriate follow-up.
4. Automated messages promote services, availability, or special offers.
5. Follow-up communication encourages appointment booking.
6. Existing clients receive reminders and re-engagement campaigns.
7. Marketing activity is tracked to help improve future campaigns.
