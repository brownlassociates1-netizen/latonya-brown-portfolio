# Salary and Earnings Dashboard

## Project Status: In Development

The Salary and Earnings Dashboard is a planned data application designed to help contractors, freelancers, and project-based workers monitor work activity, earnings, completed assignments, and expected payment dates from one clear dashboard.

## The Business Problem

People working on multiple projects often have to track assignments, hours, completion status, payment rates, and payout dates across different systems.

This can make it difficult to answer important questions:

* How much have I earned this week?
* Which projects are currently active?
* Which assignments have been completed?
* When should each payment arrive?
* How much income is still pending?
* Am I meeting my weekly income goal?

The dashboard is designed to organize this information and translate it into a simple financial overview.

## Project Goal

The goal is to create a centralized earnings-management tool that helps users understand their current work activity and expected income without relying on multiple spreadsheets, emails, and payment screens.

## Planned Features

* Project-level earnings tracking
* Active and completed project monitoring
* Hours and work-session tracking
* Weekly earnings summaries
* Expected payout dates
* Pending-payment tracking
* Payment-status updates
* Income-goal monitoring
* Project-rate comparison
* Estimated monthly income
* Historical earnings trends
* Downloadable earnings reports
* Mobile-friendly dashboard access

## Planned Dashboard Sections

### Earnings Overview

A summary of total earned, pending, paid, and projected income.

### Active Projects

A list of current projects, start dates, rates, work status, and accumulated earnings.

### Completed Work

A record of completed assignments and their payment status.

### Payment Calendar

A calendar showing expected payment dates and upcoming payouts.

### Income Goals

Progress toward weekly, monthly, and annual income targets.

### Earnings History

Charts and summaries showing changes in income over time.
